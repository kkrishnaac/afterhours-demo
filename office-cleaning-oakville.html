The offices we see here

Downtown Oakville is known for boutique offices in older buildings with character: original floors, smaller rooms and shared entrances. Along the QEW the offices are larger and more modern. Many of these offices are client-facing, with a reception area and meeting rooms that need to look their best at the start of each day. Both kinds are cleaned to the same standard, but the plan differs, and we take care in older buildings so we do not damage finishes.

Getting in and working around your building

Older downtown buildings may have a single shared entrance and limited parking. Corporate buildings by the highway usually have their own lot and entry system. Tell us which yours is and we agree access and timing at the walkthrough.
