## Fix this week

- **Cloudflare injects its Web Analytics beacon on the custom domain** (automatic setup; token in
  `data-cf-beacon`, not on workers.dev). Our CSP blocks it, so nothing is collected, but every page
  logs a CSP error in the console. The site record does not show in the dashboard yet; once it does,
  disable it (Web Analytics, the site, Manage site), or switch analytics on the project's way
  (`CF_BEACON_TOKEN`, which opens the CSP for the beacon and adds the privacy sentence).
