HARA design lab

Six alternates for review. Same logo, same navy and white, same words and photos as the live site. D, E and F (2 October) are new directions with their own type, layout and shapes. The form is a preview; the working one is on the live site.

ALight and airy

White canvas, large photo beside the headline, services as a photo strip, skyline panel for security. Mona Sans at light weights.

 BNavy band

Deep navy hero and closing band, white cards and a photo grid between them, a step timeline. Plus Jakarta Sans.

 CPhoto first

Full-screen office photo behind the headline, editorial gallery, the Toronto skyline behind the security section. Mona Sans at light weights.

 DGallery

Photography first. Edge-to-edge office photos as full-width tiles, a quiet centred headline above each, almost no cards. Hanken Grotesk.

 ELedger

Trust first. White canvas, hairline grid, square corners, a numbered index of what we clean, the week as a table. IBM Plex Sans at light weight.

 FConcierge

Hotel grade. Navy hero band with a tall photo breaking out of it, photo cards for the four priorities, square buttons, light body type. Manrope.

 GStudio

After coreastudios.com (2 October): dark canvas, blur-in on load, spectrum glare on navigation, arc dial menu, horizontal reel of uppercase photo cards, drifting gradient, live Toronto clock. Hanken Grotesk and Archivo stand in for their licensed fonts.
