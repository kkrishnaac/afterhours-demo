Any day. Any time.

Early mornings, evenings, overnight or weekends. We clean when it suits your office, and adjust the schedule whenever you need. It is planned around your opening hours and easy to change as your team grows.

  1. Book a free walkthrough. Tell us a little about your office and pick a time that suits you.

  3. You get a clear quote. A price based on what we actually saw, not a guess.
  4. Pick your schedule. Any days and times, changed whenever you need.
  5. Cleaning begins. Your checklist, followed and checked on every visit.
