Where we clean in Mississauga

  • City Centre. Offices around Hurontario Street and Square One.
  • Airport Corporate Centre. Large floor plates close to Pearson airport.
  • Meadowvale Business Park. Low-rise corporate buildings with surface parking.
  • Port Credit. Small professional offices along the lakefront and Lakeshore Road.
  • Streetsville. Main street suites in a village-scale setting.

The offices we see here

Many Mississauga offices are a single floor of a low or mid-rise building in a business park, with a larger floor area than a downtown suite and plenty of meeting rooms and kitchens. Port Credit and Streetsville are the opposite: small offices where one team uses every room. We size the plan to what we see at the walkthrough, not to a template.

Getting in and working around your building

Business park buildings usually have ground-level entrances and parking at the door, which keeps an evening or early-morning visit simple. If your building uses alarm codes or fobs, tell us at the walkthrough and we agree how they are handled before the first clean.
